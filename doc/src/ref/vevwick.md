# vevwick

## Usage
- `vevwick[expr]` gives the vacuum expectation value of the operator expression *expr*, computed with Wick's theorem.

## Details
- Wick's theorem expresses the expectation value of a product of creation and annihilation operators in a vacuum (Slater determinant or boson vacuum) as the sum over all complete pairings of the operators into contractions,
$$\langle A_1 A_2 \cdots A_{2n} \rangle = \sum_{\mathrm{pairings}} (\pm 1)^P \langle A_{i_1} A_{j_1} \rangle \langle A_{i_2} A_{j_2} \rangle \cdots \langle A_{i_n} A_{j_n} \rangle, \qquad i_m < j_m,$$
where $(-1)^P$ is the sign of the permutation that brings the paired fermion operators next to each other (no sign for bosons).
- The contractions are those of `contraction`: $\langle A B \rangle = A B - {:}A B{:}$ for the vacuum set by `ordering`. For the empty vacuum, $\langle c_i c^\dagger_j \rangle = \delta_{ij}$ and $\langle c^\dagger_i c_j \rangle = 0$; for the Fermi sea (`ordering[c] = SEA`), $\langle c^\dagger_{k\sigma} c_{k'\sigma'} \rangle = \delta_{kk'}\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}\theta(-k) = \delta_{kk'}\delta_{\sigma\sigma'} n_k$ and $\langle c_{k\sigma} c^\dagger_{k'\sigma'} \rangle = \delta_{kk'}\delta_{\sigma\sigma'}(1 - n_k)$, where the second index is the single-particle energy measured from the Fermi level.
- Only pairs of one operator with the first index `CR` and one with `AN` are contracted. Anomalous contractions $\langle c c \rangle$, $\langle c^\dagger c^\dagger \rangle$ are zero in these vacua.
- `vevwick` acts on the product after the automatic reordering by `nc`. It is linear: sums are expanded, constants are taken out, and `vevwick[sum[a, {k}]]` is `sum[vevwick[a], {k}]`. Single operators, products of an odd number of fermion operators, and normal-ordered products `dd[...]` give 0.
- The result contains `KroneckerDelta` and `UnitStep` factors. `SimplifyKD` simplifies products of them, and `sumSimplifyKD` performs sums over Kronecker deltas. `SimplifyKD` replaces $1 - \theta(-k)$ by $\theta(k)$, which is valid for $k \ne 0$; the level $k = 0$ counts as occupied ($\theta(0) = 1$) in `vev` and `vevwick`.
- For the empty vacuum, `nc` already brings products to normal order, and `vevwick` gives the same result as `vev`. `vevwick` is needed for the Fermi sea, for products of operators with `ordering[c] = NONE`, and for products that `vev` does not reduce.
- The number of complete pairings of $n$ creation and $n$ annihilation operators is $n!$. Products of six operators take about 0.01 s, products of eight operators about 0.1 s.
- `vevwick` is `Listable`.

## Developer Notes
- `vevwick[nc[...]]` calls `wickorder[n, x]` with the maximal number $n$ of contracted pairs and drops all terms with a remaining `dd[...]`. The pairs are generated by `contractedpairs`, contracted by `contract` and `contractone`, and the sign is determined by `remainder`, which commutes the contracted endpoints to the left (fermion endpoints are odd, boson endpoints even).
- `vevwicknew` and `vevwick2` are alternative implementations with the same results: `vevwicknew` only pairs operators with equal heads, and `vevwick2` returns 0 early if the numbers or heads of creation and annihilation operators do not match. They are used in the regression tests as cross-checks.

## Examples
### Basic Examples
Expectation values in the Fermi sea. The second index is the energy measured from the Fermi level, and `UnitStep[-k]` is the occupation $n_k$:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4];
vevwick[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k2, UP]]]
```
```wl
vevwick[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k2, UP], c[CR, k3, UP], c[AN, k4, UP]]]
```
`SimplifyKD` combines the terms, $\langle c^\dagger_1 c_2 c^\dagger_3 c_4 \rangle = \delta_{12}\delta_{34} n_1 n_3 + \delta_{14}\delta_{23} n_1 (1 - n_3)$, with $1 - n_3 = \theta(k_3)$:
```wl
SimplifyKD[%]
```

### Scope
For the empty vacuum (the default ordering of `d`), the result agrees with `vev`:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[d];
snegfreeindexes[i, j, k, l];
x = nc[d[AN, i, UP], d[AN, j, UP], d[CR, k, UP], d[CR, l, UP]];
{vevwick[x], vev[x]}
```
---
Boson operators, $\langle 0| a^3 (a^\dagger)^3 |0\rangle = 3!$:
```wl
snegbosonoperators[a];
vevwick[nc[a[AN], a[AN], a[AN], a[CR], a[CR], a[CR]]]
```
---
Operators of different families are not contracted with each other:
```wl
snegbosonoperators[a, b];
vevwick[nc[a[AN], b[AN], a[CR], b[CR]]]
```
---
Operators with `ordering[g] = NONE` are not reordered by `nc`; `vevwick` evaluates their products directly:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[g];
ordering[g] = NONE;
x = nc[g[AN, 1, UP], g[AN, 2, UP], g[CR, 2, UP], g[CR, 1, UP]];
{x, vevwick[x]}
```
---
Linearity and sums:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k];
snegrealfunctions[eps];
vevwick[sum[eps[k] nc[c[CR, k, UP], c[AN, k, UP]], {k}] + 1]
```

### Applications
Hartree-Fock energy. In a Slater determinant, the expectation value of the two-particle operator splits into the direct (Hartree) and the exchange (Fock) terms,
$\langle c^\dagger_{1\sigma} c^\dagger_{2\sigma'} c_{3\sigma'} c_{4\sigma} \rangle = n_1 n_2 (\delta_{14}\delta_{23} - \delta_{\sigma\sigma'} \delta_{13}\delta_{24})$:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4, s1, s2];
SimplifyKD[vevwick[nc[c[CR, k1, s1], c[CR, k2, s2], c[AN, k3, s2], c[AN, k4, s1]]]]
```
For the electron gas with $H_\mathrm{int} = \frac{1}{2V} \sum_{kpq\sigma\sigma'} V_q c^\dagger_{k+q\,\sigma} c^\dagger_{p-q\,\sigma'} c_{p\sigma'} c_{k\sigma}$, the direct term requires $q = 0$ (it cancels against the positive background), and the exchange term gives $E_\mathrm{x} = -\frac{1}{2V} \sum_{kq\sigma} V_q n_k n_{k+q}$.
---
Pair correlation in the Fermi sea. For equal spins, the exchange term cancels the direct term when both electrons are in the same level, $k_1 = k_2$ (Pauli principle); in real space, it gives the exchange hole of the pair correlation function $g_{\uparrow\uparrow}(r)$. For opposite spins, only the direct term remains:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4];
{SimplifyKD[vevwick[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[CR, k2, UP], c[AN, k3, UP], c[AN, k4, UP]]]],
 SimplifyKD[vevwick[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[CR, k2, DO], c[AN, k3, DO], c[AN, k4, UP]]]]}
```
---
Particle-hole excitations of the Fermi sea. The norm of the state $c^\dagger_{k'} c_k |\mathrm{FS}\rangle$ is $n_k (1 - n_{k'})$ for $k \ne k'$: a particle must be removed from an occupied level and added to an empty one:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k, kp];
SimplifyKD[vevwick[nc[c[CR, k, UP], c[AN, kp, UP], c[CR, kp, UP], c[AN, k, UP]]]]
```
---
Moments of the harmonic-oscillator coordinate $x = (a + a^\dagger)/\sqrt{2}$ in the ground state, $\langle x^{2n} \rangle = (2n-1)!!\, \langle x^2 \rangle^n$ (Gaussian distribution):
```wl
snegbosonoperators[a];
x = (a[AN] + a[CR])/Sqrt[2];
Table[vevwick[pow[x, n]], {n, 2, 6, 2}]
```

### Properties & Relations
`vevwick` drops all normal-ordered terms of the expansion by `wick`:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2];
x = nc[c[AN, k1, UP], c[CR, k2, UP]];
{wick[x], vevwick[x]}
```
---
`normalorderwick[x]` is `x - vevwick[x]`, and its expectation value vanishes:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4];
x = nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k2, UP], c[CR, k3, UP], c[AN, k4, UP]];
vevwick[normalorderwick[x]]
```
---
The contractions for explicit levels below ($k < 0$) and above ($k > 0$) the Fermi level:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
{vevwick[nc[c[CR, -1, UP], c[AN, -1, UP]]], vevwick[nc[c[AN, -1, UP], c[CR, -1, UP]]], vevwick[nc[c[AN, 1, UP], c[CR, 1, UP]]]}
```

### Possible Issues
The Kronecker deltas are not simplified automatically; a squared delta of the spin indexes remains:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4, s1, s2];
r = vevwick[nc[c[CR, k1, s1], c[CR, k2, s2], c[AN, k3, s2], c[AN, k4, s1]]]
```
```wl
SimplifyKD[r]
```
---
In products of boson operators and Fermi-sea operators with symbolic indexes, unphysical cross terms between boson and fermion operators remain unevaluated:
```wl
snegbosonoperators[a];
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k];
vevwick[nc[a[AN], c[CR, k, UP], c[AN, k, UP], a[CR]]]
```
They are zero; the expectation value factorizes:
```wl
vevwick[nc[a[AN], a[CR]]] vevwick[nc[c[CR, k, UP], c[AN, k, UP]]]
```
---
Spin operators are not operators of second quantization; their expectation values are not evaluated:
```wl
snegspinoperators[{S, 1/2}];
vevwick[nc[S[1, 4], S[1, 5]]]
```
