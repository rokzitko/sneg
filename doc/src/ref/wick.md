# wick

## Usage
- `wick[expr]` rewrites the operator products in *expr* as sums of normal-ordered products `dd[...]` multiplied by contractions (Wick's theorem).

## Details
- Wick's theorem for a product of operators $A_1 \cdots A_m$ reads
$$A_1 A_2 \cdots A_m = {:}A_1 \cdots A_m{:} + \sum_{(ij)} \pm \langle A_i A_j \rangle {:}\cdots{:} + \sum_{(ij)(kl)} \pm \langle A_i A_j \rangle \langle A_k A_l \rangle {:}\cdots{:} + \cdots,$$
where the sums run over all sets of one, two, ... pairs $(i < j)$, the normal-ordered products ${:}\cdots{:}$ contain the remaining operators in their original order, and the sign is the parity of the permutation of the fermion operators.
- The normal-ordered products are represented by `dd[...]`, and the contractions $\langle A_i A_j \rangle$ are computed with `contraction`. The normal order refers to the vacuum set by `ordering`: the empty state (`EMPTY`) or the Fermi sea (`SEA`).
- `wick` acts on the product after the automatic reordering by `nc`. For the empty vacuum, `nc` already gives normal order, so the expansion contains no contractions; `wick` is mainly useful for the Fermi sea and for operators with `ordering[c] = NONE`.
- Only pairs of one operator with the first index `CR` and one with `AN` are contracted.
- `wick` is linear and `Listable`, and `wick[sum[a, {k}]]` is `sum[wick[a], {k}]`. `wick[1]` is 1.
- Dropping all terms with `dd[...]` gives the expectation value; this is what `vevwick` computes.
- The number of terms grows factorially with the length of the product.

## Developer Notes
- `wick[x]` is `Sum[wickorder[n, x], {n, 0, maxcran[x]}]`, where `wickorder[n, x]` gives the terms with exactly $n$ contracted pairs and `maxcran[x]` is the smaller of the numbers of `CR` and `AN` operators. The pairs of positions are generated by `contractedpairs`, each set of pairs is contracted by `contract`, and `remainder` returns the sign and the normal-ordered remainder.

## Examples
### Basic Examples
A bilinear in the Fermi sea is its normal-ordered part plus the contraction:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4];
wick[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k2, UP]]]
```
The expansion of a product of four operators contains the fully normal-ordered term, four terms with one contraction, and the fully contracted terms:
```wl
wick[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k2, UP], c[CR, k3, UP], c[AN, k4, UP]]]
```

### Scope
For the empty vacuum, `nc` brings the product to normal order (here generating the term $\delta_{ij}$), and `wick` only marks the remaining product as normal ordered:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[d];
snegfreeindexes[i, j, k, l];
wick[nc[d[AN, i, UP], d[CR, j, UP]]]
```
---
Bosons, $a a a^\dagger a^\dagger = {:}a a a^\dagger a^\dagger{:} + 4 {:}a^\dagger a{:} + 2$. Since `nc` normal orders the product first, the result is equivalent to $a^{\dagger 2} a^2 + 4 a^\dagger a + 2$:
```wl
snegbosonoperators[a];
wick[nc[a[AN], a[AN], a[CR], a[CR]]]
```

### Applications
Normal ordering of the band Hamiltonian with respect to the Fermi sea, $H_0 = \sum_k \epsilon_k c^\dagger_k c_k = \sum_k \epsilon_k {:}c^\dagger_k c_k{:} + E_\mathrm{FS}$, with the energy of the filled Fermi sea $E_\mathrm{FS} = \sum_k \epsilon_k \theta(-k)$:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k, σ];
snegrealfunctions[eps];
wick[sum[eps[k] nc[c[CR, k, σ], c[AN, k, σ]], {k, σ}]]
```
---
Mean-field decoupling of a density-density interaction. Wick's theorem gives the exact decomposition of $c^\dagger_1 c_2 c^\dagger_3 c_4$ into the fully contracted part (Hartree-Fock energy), the terms with one contraction (the Hartree potential acting on the normal-ordered particle-hole pairs; there are no exchange terms between opposite spins), and the normal-ordered residual interaction:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4];
w = wick[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k2, UP], c[CR, k3, DO], c[AN, k4, DO]]]
```
The terms with one contraction:
```wl
Select[Expand[w], Count[#, _UnitStep, Infinity] == 1 &]
```

### Properties & Relations
Dropping the normal-ordered terms gives `vevwick`:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2, k3, k4];
x = nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k2, UP], c[CR, k3, UP], c[AN, k4, UP]];
Simplify[(wick[x] /. dd[_] -> 0) - vevwick[x]]
```
---
Since `vev[dd[...]]` is 0, `vev` of the expansion is the contraction:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2];
vev[wick[nc[c[AN, k1, UP], c[CR, k2, UP]]]]
```
---
For a bilinear, the normal-ordered product is ${:}A B{:} = A B - \langle A B \rangle$ (`normalorderwick`). Substituting it for `dd` gives back the operator product:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2];
x = nc[c[AN, k1, UP], c[CR, k2, UP]];
Expand[(wick[x] /. dd -> normalorderwick) - x]
```

### Possible Issues
`wick` is only defined for products: a single operator is returned unevaluated:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
wick[c[CR, 1, UP]]
```
---
`dd[...]` is a formal placeholder. Products of normal-ordered products are not normal ordered again; apply `wick` to the product of the operators instead:
```wl
snegfermionoperators[c];
ordering[c] = SEA;
snegfreeindexes[k1, k2];
nc[dd[nc[c[CR, k1, UP], c[AN, k1, UP]]], dd[nc[c[CR, k2, UP], c[AN, k2, UP]]]]
```
